Privacy Policy

Last updated August 3, 2026

TruePoint Digital ("we," "us," "our") operates truepoint.agency. This page explains what information we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

When you submit the form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number

We also use standard website analytics to see how the site is used (pages visited, general location, device type). This doesn't identify you personally.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, follow up about our booking and lead systems, and — if you've agreed to it — to send you SMS messages about your inquiry. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

SMS messaging

If you provide your phone number and agree to receive texts, we may send you SMS messages related to your inquiry with TruePoint Digital. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent — this information is not shared with any third parties.

How your information is stored

Contact information you submit is stored in our client management system (Go High Level), which we use to manage inquiries and follow-ups. Our website is hosted on Vercel.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we have on you at any time — just email or call us using the contact info below.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
